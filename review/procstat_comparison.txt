GETH v1.5.2 — /proc/stat delta+dt comparison vs an independent 1 Hz sampler
============================================================================
Method: an independent Python 1 Hz sampler (busy-jiffies / total-jiffies over
the measured dt, the same formula GETH's CpuSource uses) ran in parallel with
`geth --dump --count 11` on the real, idle machine (load avg 0.25). Row k is
the k-th second. GETH's tick 1 is its first LIVE tick (its baseline predates
init) and is blanked per finding 7, so it prints "n/a".
